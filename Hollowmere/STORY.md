# STORY.md — HOLLOWMERE  (canonical narrative; supersedes DESIGN §2)
# tone: uncompromising psychological horror. see HORROR_CONTENT.md.

## LOGLINE
Your daughter has been missing for three days. A music box arrives playing her voice, humming YOUR lullaby, with an address you have spent 25 years forgetting.
To bring her back you must walk through the house where you were a child — and remember what you did the night it burned.

## PROTAGONIST
NORA HALE, 34, ER trauma nurse (clinical, calm around blood — justifies her composure around gore, medical props, bandage mechanic). Single mother of MAE (6). Insomniac, avoids fire, hums a lullaby she cannot remember learning.
Voice: dry, clipped, terrified underneath. Speaks little; mostly thinks (subtitled inner monologue lines).

## BACKSTORY (revealed in layers; the player must feel each layer land)
Hollowmere = "St. Aldous Home for Children", a charity orphanage/clinic financed by the Hale family, 1991-1998.
Director DR. SILAS CRANE ran "The Quiet Programme": experimental "grief-suppression" on orphans — sensory deprivation, surgery on vocal cords and eyes, sedation, restraints, "pruning" of children who cried. 12 children, plus staff who knew and stayed silent.
NORA (age 9) and ELI (age 5, her brother) were "Hale charity wards" — placed there by their own mother, who believed the doctor's promise to cure Eli's night terrors.
Night of 3 Nov 1998: Nora found Eli in the Cellar ward. She tried to take the children out. Crane caught them. In the panic a lamp fell; fire spread. Nora reached the only exit, the heavy ward door — and LOCKED IT BEHIND HER to stop Crane following, with Eli and the children still inside. Eli screamed her name through the door. She ran.
She was found alive on the lawn, mute for a year, memory sealed ("a gap"). Her mother never spoke of it and died when Nora was 20. The Hale name quietly buried the scandal.

## THE TRUTH IN THE PRESENT (twists)
1. REVEAL A (Wing 1-2): the house is not haunted by random ghosts — it is the children, each frozen in how they were hurt. They are not evil; they are repeating the last night.
2. REVEAL B (Wing 3): Nora was a patient, not a visitor. The "gap" is a locked door in her mind (literal door motif).
3. REVEAL C (Wing 4): the Doll hunting you is ELI. He plays peek-a-boo because it was the last game Nora played with him. Every "scare" he causes is a child's hide-and-seek. Chasing/fleeing him hurts. Players must choose whether to hurt/stun him.
4. REVEAL D (Chapel): MAE is not kidnapped by Crane. The children took her — they want a "big sister" to replace the one who locked them in. Crane's remains (THE WARDEN) is trying to STOP them, because he fears what Nora's confession would do: the house exists only while the children are unforgiven and Crane keeps them "quiet". The Warden is both monster and jailer — and some of the player's sympathy flips late.
5. REVEAL E (final): the house is Nora's memory made flesh. She has been in a hospital burn unit coma for days after attempting self-harm... (soft twist, ambiguous; Mae is alive in the real world with Nora's sister). Keep ambiguous: both readings must be supportable by the journal.

## WING-BY-WING EMOTIONAL BEATS
W0 FOYER: Nora arrives, gate shuts. The house knows her name (walls whisper "Nora" in Mae's voice). Wall of children's portraits — one frame empty (Nora's). First clue: her own child's drawing on the floor — in the SAME crayon style as drawings found later, dated 1998.
W1 LIBRARY: records room. Admission files. Cipher = the staff's code for "treatments". First file reads "Subject 9: HALE, NORA". Player realises she was a patient. Ada's tragedy. Sigil 1 = ADA'S RIBBON. Choice: burn Crane's files (lose evidence) or keep them (needed for true ending).
W2 CONSERVATORY: the "therapy garden"; children's graves disguised as flowerbeds, tags with names; Mr. Pell's pruning room (gore: restraints, tools). Hollow Man chase. Sigil 2 = GARDEN KEY (a child's tooth on a ring).
W3 BOILER CELLAR / CHORUS WARD: flooded, steam, the singing children. Shortest, most body-horror wing. Choice: save a trapped ghost child (costs oil/time) or skip. Sigil 3 = THE CHORD (a tuning fork bent like a spine).
W4 NURSERY/ATTIC: Eli. Heart of the game. Hide-and-seek set piece lasting 10+ min: you must find Eli's pieces while he counts. Revelation: Eli's "monster" is a boy who wants his sister back. Sigil 4 = ELI'S BLANKET. Strongest cry-or-scream moment. Remove jumpscares for 5 minutes during the emotional core — silence & lullaby only.
W5 CHAPEL: Mae on the altar surrounded by children. Warden breaks the door. Final chase through the burning/collapsing house = recreation of the 1998 escape, but now the player CHOOSES at the ward door: lock it again (repeat sin) or open it (go back for them).

## ENDINGS (5, driven by tracked choices; see GAMEPLAY §MORALITY)
E1 THE DOOR (best/"true"): open ward door, confess aloud (player-timed speech minigame), children forgive, the house burns away, Nora wakes beside Mae. Requires: all sigils, ≥10/12 fragments, never harmed a child-ghost, kept files.
E2 THE LULLABY (good): Nora stays to sing the children to rest, Mae walks out alone. Bittersweet; Nora implied to die.
E3 THE LOCK (bad): she locks the door again; escapes with Mae; Mae never speaks again; final shot: Nora hums the lullaby with Eli's voice.
E4 THE REPLACEMENT (horror): fails final choice — Nora joins the children as "Subject 9" forever, Mae taken as new sister (dark twist final image).
E5 ASH (secret): Warden's POV — play 90 s as Crane in the fire; unlock after beating the game on Nightmare; reframes the villain with a final line.

## NARRATIVE DELIVERY RULES
- Environmental first: drawings, medical charts, locked doors mirrored from memory.
- Notes (40) written in 3 voices: Crane's clinical logs (chilling), Ada/children's drawings & short scrawls, Nora's adult internal monologue. Each note ≤ 90 words.
- 12 MEMORY FRAGMENTS: playable flashbacks (child Nora, shorter height, mono-colour red tint, 60-90 s each) triggered by touching objects (a shoe, a doll, a key). These are the emotional spine. Each ends on a cut + a sound, never a cheap scare.
- Recurring images: the number 9, the red crayon, lullaby motif "HUSH LITTLE LIGHT" (original, 8 bars, minor, 3/4), doors closing, counting ("one, two, three—").
- Foreshadow twists in Foyer (signature in visitor book: "N. Hale, 1998").
- Mae's voice is used sparingly: 14 total lines, always subtitled; the engine must NEVER repeat the same line twice in a 10-min span.
- Humour: none. Warmth: small and precious (a crayon drawing "mama + me + light").
- Pacing: 8 hooks per hour — a hook = reveal, scare, or beautiful moment; Director task T-081 tracks.

## INTRO MOVIE SCRIPT (animated, 90-110 s) — replaces DESIGN §7 beats
1. Black. Child's voice counting "one, two, three…" over a heartbeat. Fire-orange flicker on a closed door. Cut black.
2. Rain. Nora's hospital: she wipes blood from her gloves, sees a voicemail icon: Mae's school "Mae is not here". Phone drops.
3. Music box on her kitchen table; her own lullaby plays, wrong tempo; Mae's voice sings the last line. Envelope: black wax stamp "H".
4. Rain road, car, a gate; Hollowmere in lightning flashes; windows light one by one as if watching.
5. Close on Nora's hand on the cold gate; it clangs shut behind (sound only, screen cuts). Title HOLLOWMERE glitches; subtitle "Some doors should stay locked". Fade into first-person at the front door.
Style: paper-cut silhouettes, high contrast, crimson accent only on fire/crayon/blood.
